Our craft

Light is a material. We treat it like one.

We started LUMO because most lamps get one thing wrong: the light itself. Ours is tuned toward the warm end of the spectrum, dims without flicker, and renders skin and wood and paper the way daylight would at dusk. The rest — the shade, the stem, the switch — is built to be repaired, not replaced.

  • Warmth you can tune

    Every lamp dims from a 2200K candle-glow to a crisp 3000K reading light — no cold blue, ever.

  • Honest materials

    Spun aluminium, powder-coated steel and mouth-blown opal glass — finished by hand, meant to patina well.

  • Built to be repaired

    Standard bulbs, replaceable drivers and a five-year warranty. Send us a part, not the whole lamp.
