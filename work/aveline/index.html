The house of Aveline

Considered design,
honest materials.

Aveline was founded on a quiet idea: a wardrobe of fewer, finer things. Each piece is designed to outlast a season — cut from natural fibres, made in small runs by ateliers we know by name.

No logos, no noise. Just clothes that hold their shape, soften with wear, and look as considered in ten years as they do today.
