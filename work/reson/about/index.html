Our philosophy

We tune by ear, not by graph.

RESON started with a frustration: audio that measured perfectly and sounded lifeless. So we do it the slow way — every product is voiced over months in an anechoic room until it sounds like the room the music was made in, not a flat line on a chart.

No sub-brands, no yearly gimmick. Just a small lineup, each product earning its place, each one better a year after you buy it.

How it's built

Machined, not moulded.

Aluminium yokes, replaceable pads and cables, gaskets you can service. Firmware that adds features instead of forcing an upgrade. We build audio to be repaired — not landfilled — and back it for two years.
