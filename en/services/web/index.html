FAQ

Four of the most common questions customers ask before building a website

How much does it cost to be an international B2B website?

All offers are assessed on a needs-based basis. All offers are confirmed in writing at the programme stage and are confirmed without price.

Why do you write static instead of WordPress?

Handwritten static stations do not have plugins and database burdens, and are usually open for global CDNs in 0.1-2 seconds, with a clean structure for Google entries, and no security risks from plug-in gaps. If there is a real need for frequent self-help updates of a large amount of content, a clean CMS + static-generated programme is recommended. See the detailed analysis.Resource Classroom。

How long is the typical site build cycle?

The basic network of officials usually lasts 2-4 weeks, while the independent station for foreign trade usually lasts 4-8 weeks, depending on the number of pages, languages and content preparation.

Who owns the website when it's done?

It's all yours. Deliver all web source and design files, domain names and hosts are registered in your name, without technical binding, and can be handed over to any team for maintenance at any time.
