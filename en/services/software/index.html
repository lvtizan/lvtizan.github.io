Our approach

Professional software is not a consumer app with more buttons

Consumer-level design is popular, and professional software design is "fast and steady for professionals." Both approaches do.

01

Information density: one screen view full

Users of specialized software are skilled operators: readings, states, tools, support lines are to be present at the same time, reducing round-to-back switching — over-whiteing is less efficient.

02

Designed for Proficiency

Consumption App Optimizing First Use, Professional Software Optimizing Hundreds of times a day: Fast Paths, Remuneible Fixed Layout, Hardware Key Maps to give old hands a muscle memory.

03

Environment and Fault Tolerance

Great light field, gloves, single-hand operations — high-comparison colour, big enough click areas, critical operations double-checked. One error could be a day of data.

Only need to design, no need to develop, can we cooperate?

Yes. The design + One project is designed to be carried out by both the pure design project (delivery of prototypes, high security designs, design systems and labels) and the single project. The pure design project will be used to export standard documents from your development team to ensure landing.

How much does the software interface design cost?

The software and applications are quoted on a needs assessment basis: the main variable is the number of interfaces, complexity and inclusion of development.
