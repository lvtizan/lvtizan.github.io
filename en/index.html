Q&A//Common Key Inquiries

Frequently asked questions about custom software development and delivery

Direct answers on ownership, maintainability, timelines and the practical details clients care about most.

How does business customize software development ensure 100 per cent ownership of source code and digital asset sovereignty?
YOYANT insists on 100% full source code and database privatization delivery. The delivery consists of a complete Git version library and submission record from the initial construction, a front-end and back-end project without any binary encryption, a full database DDL script, a one-key deployment file for Docker containerization and a Figma high-security design source file. Signing a legally valid full-volume transfer protocol without any encrypted black box or authorization to lock down.
What is the difference between software customization development and the low-code platform on the market, the SaaS template?
SaaS templates and low-code platforms usually charge expensive annual fees every year, data are blocked by the manufacturer, cannot be freely exported or in deep, and often cannot be cleared through the apple application market due to homogenization. Customization development is the construction of an exclusive system for your core business model, where data are fully privatized on your own cloud server, supporting the sinking of any customized business logic and privatization algorithms, and no follow-up platform is drawn into kidnapping with the manufacturer.
How long does the commercial App, Micro-Intelligent Business City and the management backstage usually last?
Pre-cooperative parties will confirm in writing the precise milestones that include prototype tracking, development alignment, as well as greyscale testing and deployment delivery.
Can we take over successfully after delivery if we need internal team maintenance or handover?
Perfectly. We adhere strictly to the rules of the large plant module decoupling structure and the TypeScript/Swift/Kotlin powerful type of engineering, providing standardized OpenAPI/Swagger interfaces, CI/CD automated flow-line scripts and delivery training videos recorded by the master of the main architecture. Any regular engineer can quickly take over the compilation and operation within 1-2 days, without any third-party maintenance dependency.
