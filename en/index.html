The ownership principle

No black boxes. No hostage code.

Your software is an operating asset. We deliver it so your team can run, inspect, extend and redeploy it without asking our permission.

01

Complete source and database delivery

Frontend, backend, database schemas, design sources, deployment configuration and version history are handed over as a coherent whole.

02

Infrastructure in your name

Domains, cloud accounts, payment accounts and data remain under your organisation's control, with private-cloud deployment available.

03

Senior people stay involved

You work directly with practitioners who understand architecture, interface design and business workflows—without a sales-to-junior relay.

04

A maintainable handover

Typed code, modular architecture, API contracts, deployment scripts and training make the product practical for an internal or future team to own.

Questions, answered

Before we build together.

Do we own the source code and intellectual property?

Yes. The agreed deliverables include readable source code, database schemas, design sources and deployment assets. Ownership transfer is written into the engagement, with no licence lock or encrypted black box.

Can you work with an overseas company?

Yes. We work remotely in English or Chinese, document decisions asynchronously and can deploy to global infrastructure such as AWS and other client-owned cloud environments.

How long does a custom product take?

A focused application or commerce experience often takes 2–8 weeks. Industrial integrations and larger multi-role platforms are scheduled after discovery because hardware, compliance and workflow depth materially change the scope.

Can our internal team maintain the product later?

That is the goal. We favour typed, modular engineering, explicit API contracts and reproducible deployments, then provide documentation and handover support so another qualified team can take over.
