I. Three Typical Pitfalls of the Software Customization Market

Sales outsourcing broker and multi-layer subcontracting (the “transmittal” scenario)

The overwhelming majority of mid-to-low-end outsourcing companies in the market are essentially"Sales-driven leather bag company"In the pre-sales phase, business is connected to the customer, who says that any demand is “no problem at all, and it'll be on the line next month”.

Once the contract is signed, the business will draw 60% ~70% of the commission on the demand, subcontracting the project to a team of students lacking practical engineering experience, or to a cheap part-time job. Without a architect who closes the chain of technical feasibility to business, the product has been developed in a confusing logic, and the Bug series. The final communication costs are extremely high, and the project will inevitably be delayed.

💡 Identification Signal

If the answer is always “Go back and ask about the technology”, you can see that you are dealing with a sales broker.

2. "Formwork housing" disguised as "full customization"

Some service providers claim that “thousands of dollars of custom-made proprietary motion app/ malls” are actually delivered to customers with old, open-source templates (even with serious SQL injections and XSS leaks) that were purchased on the Internet a few years ago.

Such software codes are extremely swollen and filled with tens of thousands of lines of unmaintained dead codes, and not only cannot pass Apple Store's aerial and manual review, but the bottom structure is simply unsupported and completely overturns the reconstruction once the follow-up business requires the expansion of the core logic of multi-ware storage, stairwell prices, real-time network communications, etc.

3. Source-code encryption and black box binding (loss of sovereignty over digital assets)

This is the most hidden and lethal commercial trap.

  • Core business logic packaged into crypto obfuscated .so / .jar Pre-compiled dynamic libraries;
  • The database does not provide a complete creation statement (DDL) and external key constraint, but only an export file with an outsourcer ' s authorization to verify;
  • The code contains requests for cardiac arrest rights sent to the contractor's private server, and the system is automatically locked to death once the high annual maintenance fee has been stopped.

III. Business Software Customization Core Architecture Selection Decision Tree

The requirements for performance, development cycle and hardware interaction are different from one business pattern.

1. Business movement App: Native (Swift/Kotlin) or Transverse (Flutter)?

  • Prefer original (SwiftUI / Combine / Jetpack Company):When your product involves industrial-grade sensor interaction, continuous data acquisition, high-precision camera frame processing, or very high frame graphic rendering. VeinScope , it is important to ensure the stability of the millisecond level of the bottom data interaction.
  • Prioritize cross-platform (Flutter / React Nature):When you need to go online on the iOS and Android side at the same time, pursue a single brand of UI vision, core focus on data presentation, community interaction, or trade flows. AVATAR, Flutter can achieve a full 60fps flow effect with very high cost-effectiveness.

2. Micro-trust ecology: a primary applet or a multi-end framework?

A micro-trust applet is limited to 2MB primary package volume and strict auditing standards. If business requires a split between the write-off line, complex multispect commodity SKU step price and tens of millions of tiers of funds, a rigorous subcontract preloading mechanism must be designed and locks such as anti-repetitive submission (e.g. Epaifa Cross Border Mall）。

3. Business management medium backstage: Vue 3 / React + TypeScript + Atomic Service Lock

The middle backstage is not just “a few tables plus several bullet windows”, but is a nerve hub that supports the operation of the entire business. It must have a fine particle size RBAC permission control, multiple store atom write-off (avoiding high and oversale), real-time log audit and a drillable BI data screen.
