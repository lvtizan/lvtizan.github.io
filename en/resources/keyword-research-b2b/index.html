Most common mistake: use your word, not the buyer's word

Foreign traders make keywords, and eight or nine of them are in the same pit:Use your own product terminology, internal model, and even Chinese English as keywords.You call it a "high-precision control counter" every day, and you put it in a title in English, and you think it's professional -- but the overseas buyer might have hit it in Google. cnc rotary table supplier or 4th axis rotary table for millingYou and the buyer are in two sets of languages, and the words don't match, and they don't look at him in the face.

The whole meaning of keyword research is"How do you describe the product" is translated into "How do the buyer search the product"?Don't start with the product instructions, with the situation of a buyer: what's his problem, what's he going to buy and how he'll talk about it?

2. See what words your peers stand on independently

Find a few good foreign colleagues and see what their product page titles, H1 and URLs use. They can line up and show that they're both search-in and product-in-the-post, and they can copy their ideas.

3. Keyword Planner/Paid Tool

Google Ads' keyword planners can see the size of the search and the level of competition (with an advertising account). The budget is enough for Ahrefs, Semrush, to read the volume, the difficulty, the words. Tools are acceleration, not a precondition -- many good words are enough by the two above.
