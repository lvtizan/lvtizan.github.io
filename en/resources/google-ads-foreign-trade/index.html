How to calculate the account: backward from "how much is a customer worth"

Clicks x Inquiry Conversion Rate = Number of enquiries
Ad Spend ÷ Number of Inquiries = Cost per Enquiry
Number of inquiries × Closing rate × Profit per order = Profit from advertising
