Project Overview · Overview

Epaifa Mall is a supply chain created for cross-border wholesalers + integrated commercial mobile application. It divides the past between purchases, warehouses, sales, and finance, and absorbs a mobile phone: a single-key purchase order for the boss, multiple warehouse receipts, a case-by-case count and a bad goods belt report, automatic replenishment at stock levels; a business-side pick-up warehouse, dispatching task and cross-border lead-tracking; a billing line at the end of the transaction; a closing order (cargo / VAT / COD/ freight) + cash collection and outstanding reconciliation; and a refund-back loop.

The stock is in the middle of two chains, with a two-way drive-up upstream replenishment and downstream performance, creating a retroactive business closed loop.
