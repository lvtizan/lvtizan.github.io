Quality Inspection Process

What records to keep for each session

It's not "we value quality," it's what documents are produced at every step, how long are they kept, if you want them.

IQC

Incoming Inspection

A check of the furnace number and the material certificate, both hard and thick, for each batch. The record is three years.

FAI

First item inspection

Each new piece number, the first report after each remodel, is measured at three coordinates of full size, before the client's confirmation is made.

IPQC

Process inspection

Check critical dimensions every 2 hours, SPC records Cpk. Over and above the control line automatic stop.

OQC

Shipment inspection

Sample AQL 0.65, with export inspection reports and packing photographs.
