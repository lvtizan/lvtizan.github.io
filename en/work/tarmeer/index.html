Project Overview · Overview

Tarmeer is a vertical station for interior design and building materials that places "owners looking for companies, companies looking for clients, and parties choosing building materials" on a platform. For UAE and Viet Nam, a multilingual national structure: owners can screen companies by city and type, browse the collection of real works and ask questions directly; suppliers / Refurbishers can register to build their own exhibition halls (corporate information, business licences, product catalogues, project cases) one step at a backstage, and manage the questionnaires received (internal Query CRM, Chinese/English bilingual).

One platform, three interconnected processes

The difficulty for Tarmeer is that the different players do their work in the same product set: the owners look for partners, the merchants set up a credible exhibition hall, and the platform manages content and clues on an ongoing basis.

01 / DISCOVERY

Let the owner find the right company and materials from the needs

The portal is separated by company, expert and building materials and the catalogue is then screened by city and business type.

02 / SHOWROOM

The show hall brings together the work, the product and the contact information.

The design company and the construction material supplier need to display different content, but the visitors are interested in qualifications, past projects, and what to offer. The exhibition hall shares the company’s presentation, project cases, and product content organization on the same path, and then puts advisory action at the decision point.

03 / OPERATIONS

Self-service back-office onboarding, content maintenance, and enquiries

Businesses improve the content of the information and exhibition halls in a step-by-step manner, and the back office concentrates on the management of products, projects and queries received.

Cross-market information architecture

UAE does not simply copy into translation pages for cities, companies and content with the Vietnamese market.

Architecture projects

so that the multi-role platform can continue to expand

The design community will define the boundaries of the market, content, and clues. Otherwise, new cities, businesses, and languages will be re-engineered from the front and back.

Market and Language Stratification

The State determines the attribution of the city, the company and the content; the language determines how the same business information is presented.

Unified showroom content structure

The business maintains company information, projects and products in the back, the front desk catalogue and the business hall to read the same set of business content.

Enquiry is traceable

When a visitor initiates contact from a catalogue or exhibition hall, the associated company is kept in the context of the page, and the back office returns the trail to the business so that the operator can know where the demand comes from and who should handle it.
