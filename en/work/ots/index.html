Project Issue · The Brief

The client needs to judge in a short time: whether the project will cover the target market, who will cooperate with the technology, how the manufacture and shipment will be connected, and how the installation of the commissioning and follow-up services will land.

As a result, the OTS network does not follow the usual order of “brand-story list”, but rather builds an overall understanding of project delivery capacity and answers the real concerns of the project purchasers on a step-by-step basis through global networks, manufacturing bases, on-site operations, project reference and project queries.

Information strategy

Prove “deliverable” before discussing “what to sell”

OTS does not make the first page into a catalogue of products. The page is organized in the order of risk to the purchaser, so that each rolling down answers a more specific project question.

01 / CAPABILITY

Establish scale and boundaries first

The first screen does not speak of a single model, defining the range of capabilities in terms of “global R & D, Chinese manufacturing, international delivery, full-cycle services,” and then using numbers, networks and manufacturing bases to bring abstract commitments to verifiable information.

02 / EXECUTION

Turn installation into a visible process

Installation is not a service at the end of the page. It is broken down into field teams, standardized nodes, quality control and safety prerequisites, and is linked to a continuous project chain with manufacturing, logistics, debugging.

03 / EVIDENCE

Reduce decision making costs with similar projects

You can quickly judge whether you have made a complex scene close to yourself.

Make progress

Instead of drawing a page first, we'll sort out the order of the buyer's judgement.

The translation of complex operations into a clear, credible network structure that allows for queries begins with information that the project has a real need for confirmation.

01 / DISCOVER

Dismantling engineering decisions

From project location, type of building, wellways and technical parameters, to shipment, installation and protection, key issues in buyer decision-making are included as the page skeleton.

02 / STRUCTURE

Restructuring Trusted Information

The global network, manufacturing and design centres, on-site operations and project information are placed in their respective positions, and the boundaries of the commitment to marking services and the project reference are clearly identified.

03 / DESIGN

Restrained engineering

Quiet Engineering in deep blue, bone white and high-comparison information levels makes the sense of order for large projects a page quality.

04 / SHIP

Let content be found

Synchronizes the visible content of the page into structured data, site maps and shared cards; forms, accessability and photo loadings are for real transformation.

01 / PROJECT NARRATIVE

First explain the project delivery, then introduce the ability

From global R & D, Chinese manufacturing to installation services, the pages are organized in the order of judgement of the project purchaser, rather than being branded or stacked.

02 / REFERENCE LIBRARY

Make case base comparable and searchable

The three types of projects, airfields, orbital traffic and public buildings, are listed together with the number of country, site maps and equipment, which gives a good sense of similar delivery experience.

Close browsing to followable item input

01 / MANUFACTURING EVIDENCE

It's not a "high power" line. It's a set of verifiable delivery information.

Buyers can check the scale of production, location and supply tempo without jumping back and forth.

02 / GLOBAL PROJECT LIBRARY

Casebook organized by project scene instead of a simple stack of project photos

The three lines of airport, orbital traffic, and public buildings are presented in their entirety.

03 / LIFECYCLE DELIVERY

Translating "China Supply" into a full-cycle delivery commitment with specific overseas projects

Instead of an abstract set of business presentations, the project phase, the source of supply and the continuity of the service are presented in the same narrative as evidence of the delivery of the building.

04 / RESPONSIBILITY CHAIN

Who's in charge of what from design, manufacturing to installation?

The service plate breaks down the system design, manufacture, international logistics, installation and services into four units with clear lines of responsibility, avoiding the expression “all can do” without a path of implementation.

05 / PROJECT ENQUIRY

The Queries page does not just leave contact details, but collects conditions that advance the project first.

Fields are organized around project countries and cities, building types, contact persons and project requirements. This allows information obtained by a sales or project team to enter the program directly, rather than re-examining it from a vague message.
